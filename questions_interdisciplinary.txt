# Interdisciplinary research questions for preference-pair collection.
#   sbatch --array=0-N%C scripts/slurm/run_pipeline_batch.sbatch questions_interdisciplinary.txt SLICE_COUNT
#
# Written to a deliberate shape, following the 2026-09-07 benchmark finding that
# a vague data_requirement ("a public dataset") sends dataset discovery matching
# on a single word and attaching an irrelevant corpus. Each question below implies
# a CONCRETE and COMMON data type — tabular records, a labelled text corpus, or a
# univariate/multivariate time series — so the resolved input has a fair chance of
# being the right one. Each also names a specific comparison, so the Experiment
# Planner has a baseline to plan against rather than an open-ended exploration.

# --- Finance x machine learning -------------------------------------------------
Do transformer-based sequence models outperform classical ARIMA baselines at forecasting daily volatility of equity index returns once transaction costs are accounted for?
Does sentiment extracted from financial news headlines improve next-day directional prediction of stock returns beyond price-derived features alone?
Do gradient-boosted decision trees retain their accuracy advantage over neural networks on tabular credit-risk data as the training set grows past one hundred thousand records?
How much does the choice of class-imbalance correction — resampling, class weighting, or decision-threshold tuning — change fraud-detection recall at a fixed false-positive budget?
Does adding macroeconomic indicators as auxiliary prediction targets in a multi-task model improve credit default prediction over an equivalent single-task model?
Can market regimes identified by unsupervised clustering of price and volume features improve the out-of-sample risk-adjusted return of a simple momentum rule?
Does feature drift in consumer lending data degrade model calibration faster than it degrades ranking discrimination?
Are ensemble-based uncertainty estimates for asset return models predictive of realised forecast error, or merely of input variance?
Does quantile regression give better tail-risk estimates than a Gaussian error assumption on daily returns of individual equities?

# --- Health and biology x machine learning --------------------------------------
Does adding patient demographic features to clinical time-series models genuinely improve early deterioration prediction, or mainly encode dataset-specific bias?
Can dimensionality reduction on gene-expression data preserve enough signal for tissue-type classification using a small fraction of the original features?
How does label noise in clinical annotations affect the calibration of a classifier's confidence relative to its accuracy?
Do survival models outperform binary classifiers for predicting hospital readmission when the follow-up window varies between patients?
Does sequence length of electronic health record histories affect predictive accuracy more than the choice of model architecture?

# --- Climate, energy and environment x machine learning -------------------------
Can gradient-boosted models trained on historical weather records forecast seasonal crop yield more accurately than a simple degree-day growth model?
Does including spatial autocorrelation as an explicit feature improve air-quality prediction over models using temporal features alone?
How much does the temporal resolution of input measurements affect accuracy in short-horizon electricity demand forecasting?
Can unsupervised anomaly detection on sensor time series identify equipment degradation earlier than fixed-threshold alarms?
Does transfer learning from data-rich weather stations improve forecasting at stations with short observational histories?

# --- Economics and social science x machine learning ----------------------------
Does policy uncertainty derived from news text correlate with subsequent regional changes in unemployment more strongly than lagged unemployment alone?
Can clustering of household survey responses recover income strata more reliably than predefined income thresholds?
Do network centrality measures computed on trade flows predict economic shock propagation better than bilateral trade volume alone?
Does the choice of fairness constraint materially change which applicants a lending model rejects, at equal overall accuracy?

# --- Language and text x other disciplines --------------------------------------
Does domain-specific vocabulary adaptation improve named-entity recognition in legal and regulatory text more than simply increasing model size?
How does the readability of a source document affect the factual accuracy of extractive summarisation of it?
Can cross-lingual transfer substitute for in-language training data in low-resource sentiment classification, and at what data ratio?
Does the length of a document affect classification accuracy more than its vocabulary richness?
Can topic models trained on scientific abstracts identify emerging interdisciplinary research areas earlier than citation-based measures?

# --- Physical sciences x machine learning ---------------------------------------
Can graph-based molecular representations predict compound solubility better than fingerprint-based features at an equal parameter budget?
Does active learning reduce the number of simulations needed to map a materials property landscape compared with random sampling?
Do physics-informed loss terms improve extrapolation beyond the training range in regression on experimental measurements?

# --- Methodology questions that cut across domains -------------------------------
Do probability calibration methods validated on balanced benchmarks transfer to heavily imbalanced real-world tabular data?
Does feature-selection stability across resampled datasets correlate with downstream generalisation performance?
How much of the reported benefit of data augmentation on tabular data survives when the evaluation split respects temporal ordering?
Does model performance ranking on a small validation set reliably predict ranking on a held-out test set of the same distribution?
Do simpler interpretable models match black-box accuracy on tabular tasks once hyperparameter search budgets are equalised?
