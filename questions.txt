When an instruction-tuned model (e.g., Gemma 2B) explains its reasoning in chain-of-thought, how closely do the attention heads activated during the explanation correlate with the circuits that generated the initial hidden-state representation?
How does 2-bit vs. 4-bit quantization (e.g., via GGUF/AWQ) alter or erase specific semantic sparse autoencoder (SAE) features in the middle layers of small LLMs?
Can steering/persona vectors targeting refusal mechanisms be isolated and surgically disabled using activation patching without degrading general task performance?
Which linear, CPU-bound heuristics (e.g., token-to-type ratios, perplexity spikes, syntactic entropy) are most effective at predicting and removing uninformative samples from fine-tuning datasets before running any training?
How does training a small model (under 3B parameters) on synthetic datasets generated by closed models introduce systematic blind spots or stylistic hallucinations compared to training on human-annotated datasets?
Does tracking token-level output entropy and logit variance across consecutive layers serve as a reliable real-time indicator of factual hallucinations during pure inference?
How severely do lightweight encoder models (e.g., DeBERTa-v3) drop in performance on structured tasks (NER, relation extraction) when evaluated on non-standard orthography or regional dialects vs. standard dialect benchmarks?
What is the minimal set of linear projection matrices (Q,K,V,O vs. MLP layers) required for LoRA fine-tuning to successfully teach a small model specialized domain jargon without causing catastrophic forgetting on general benchmarks?
Does low-bit quantization disproportionately degrade performance in morphologically rich or agglutinative low-resource languages compared to morphologically simple languages due to subword tokenization splits?
Can gradient reversal techniques on lightweight adapter layers selectively erase specific copyrighted or sensitive concepts from a 1B-3B model faster and with less compute than fine-tuning on negative examples?
