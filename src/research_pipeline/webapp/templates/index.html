Papers

Used in place of the search. Only title is required — authors, abstract, year, doi and pdf_url are used when present. Papers with no title are dropped.

Without this, the Experiment Planner takes whichever hypothesis the ranking put first. With it, the run stops after the Hypothesis Agent, shows you all three with their ranks and scores, and continues to the stage you picked above once you have chosen.

A full run searches for papers, synthesizes hypotheses, plans and executes experiments, then drafts and reviews a paper — expect tens of minutes. Stopping earlier is proportionally faster: the run ends after the stage you pick, keeping every output produced up to there. Blank fields use the configured defaults.
